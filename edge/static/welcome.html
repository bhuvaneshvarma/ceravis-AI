Connect the internet by cable

  1. Take a network (Ethernet) cable.
  2. Plug one end into a free LAN port of the home router — usually numbered 1–4, not the one marked WAN or Internet.
  3. Plug the other end into the Ethernet port of this device.

Nothing to set up: the router gives the device its address by itself, and this check turns green within a few seconds. The device's Wi-Fi stays the cameras' own hotspot, which is why the internet comes by cable.
