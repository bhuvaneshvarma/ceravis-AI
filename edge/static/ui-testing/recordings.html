How this console works

Explained in the simplest way — every button, and what happens behind it.

The two modes

🔴 Live
Shows what the camera sees right now, with almost no delay (under a second). It uses the same "live link" the CERAVIS cloud app uses. Think of it as a window into the room this very moment.
◷ Playback
Lets you go back in time and watch what was already recorded. You get a play/pause button and a slider, exactly like watching a saved video.

The camera list (left side)

Every camera set up on this device. A green dot means it is online and streaming; grey means it is not sending video. Click one to select it.

The green timeline (bottom, in Playback)

Picture a ruler that shows the last 12 hours. The device does not record all day — it only records when a person is seen. So the ruler is mostly empty, with green blocks where footage exists. Click any green block to jump straight to that moment. The white line is where you are currently watching.

Why is most of the timeline empty?

On purpose. Recording only "who was in frame" keeps storage tiny and makes review fast — you are never scrubbing through hours of an empty room. Old footage older than 12 hours deletes itself automatically.

Play / pause and the skip buttons

► plays or pauses. ⏮ / ⏭ jump 15 seconds back or forward. Jump to latest takes you to the most recent recorded moment.

Where does the video come from?

Nothing is uploaded to the internet. Live video and recordings both come straight from the device on your network. When you watch from outside the home, it travels through the secure tunnel — but only while you are actually watching.

If a video will not load

There is nothing to configure — this page works out the right addresses from the one it was opened at. Two things can still stop a video: (1) the media backbone chip at the top is red — the streaming engine is down; restart the device service. (2) On the home network only, secure streaming may need its certificate trusted once — the page shows you the exact link to open and approve.

Is this permanent?

No. This whole page is an optional add-on kept in its own folder. It can be removed at any time with zero effect on cameras, recording, alerts, or the cloud app.
